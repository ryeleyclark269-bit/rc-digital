Questions

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs benefit from a groom every 6 to 8 weeks. Curly and long coats may need it every 4 to 6 weeks.

Does my dog need to be vaccinated?

Yes. We ask that all dogs are up to date with their vaccinations for everyone's safety.

My dog is nervous. Can you help?

Absolutely. We groom one dog at a time, take breaks whenever needed and never rush. Let us know when you book.

How do I pay?

Cash or card on collection.
