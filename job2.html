Home Improvements Website

A professional, sleek website for a local remodelling and repairs company, built to onboard new clients and bring in new jobs.

The brief

This local company relied on word of mouth and had no simple way for new customers to find them or ask for a quote.

They needed a website that looked as professional as their work and made it easy for homeowners to get in touch.

What we delivered

  • Quote request formNew clients can describe their job and request a quote online.
  • Services pagesClear pages for remodelling, repairs and maintenance work.
  • Project galleryBefore-and-after photos that show the quality of the work.
  • Mobile-first designLooks sharp and loads fast on every phone.

The result

Onboarding new clients is now simple: enquiries arrive with the details already filled in, helping the team find and book new jobs faster.
