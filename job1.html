Job Docket App

An app we designed and built to take the paperwork out of the trades. Jobs, photos, receipts, invoices and profit, all in one place on your phone.

The idea

Tradespeople lose hours every week to paperwork: writing up jobs, keeping receipts, building invoices and working out what they actually made. JobDocket brings all of that into one simple app, built to be used on site.

Inside the app

Key features

  • Job recordsLog client details and the work for every job.
  • Before & after photosCapture photos of the job straight from the camera.
  • AI job summariesTurn a quick note into a professional write-up.
  • Receipts & costsUpload receipts so materials are added to the job automatically.
  • Invoice builderLabour, materials, VAT and discounts in a few taps.
  • Email to clientSend the finished docket or invoice straight to the customer.
  • Profit dashboardSee revenue, costs and profit by month and by trade.
  • Team accountsInvite team members to work from the same account.
