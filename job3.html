Luma Skin Studio Website

An elegant website for a local aesthetics studio, showing off their treatments and photos of recent work.

The brief

Luma Skin Studio wanted a website that matched the calm, premium feel of their studio.

It needed to present their services clearly and let the results speak for themselves through photos of recent treatments.

What we delivered

  • Treatment menuEvery service with a clear description and pricing.
  • Results galleryPhotos of recently completed treatments.
  • Booking enquiriesClients can request an appointment straight from the site.
  • On-brand designSoft tones and refined typography to match the studio.

The result

The studio now has a site that reflects its brand, showcases its work, and turns visitors into booked appointments.
