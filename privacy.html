Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who we are

RC Digital ("we", "us", "our") is a website, app and digital marketing business based in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at rcdigitaluk@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, including:

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, business name and anything else you include when you email us.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address and the date you asked to join.
  • Client projects: contact details, billing details and the content you send us for your website or app.

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising tools. Our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in server logs to keep the site secure and running.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver and support the work you have asked us to do.
  • To send our newsletter, offers and updates, only if you have signed up.
  • To keep business and accounting records we are required to keep by law.

4. Our legal reasons for using it

  • Consent: for our newsletter and marketing emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: when we need your information to provide a quote or carry out work for you.
  • Legitimate interests: to reply to enquiries and run our business.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records.

5. Newsletter and marketing emails

We only send marketing emails to people who have asked to receive them. Every email includes a simple way to unsubscribe, or you can email rcdigitaluk@gmail.com with the subject "Unsubscribe" and we will remove you straight away.

6. Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with services we use to run our business, such as:

  • Google (Gmail), which we use for email.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • An email newsletter provider, if we use one to send our newsletter.

Some of these providers may store information outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 12 months.
  • Client and billing records: up to 6 years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email rcdigitaluk@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
