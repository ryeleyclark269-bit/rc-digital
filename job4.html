North & Oak Café Website

A warm, welcoming website for a lovely local café that wanted to grow its reach in the community.

The brief

North & Oak had a loyal local following but wanted to improve their outreach and reach new customers.

They chose RC Digital to give them an online presence that feels as cosy as the café itself.

What we delivered

  • Online menuEasy-to-update menu for food, drinks and specials.
  • Opening hours & locationFind-us details with an embedded map.
  • Social media linksConnected to their Instagram and Facebook.
  • Events & newsA space to promote events and seasonal offers.

The result

The café now has a home online that brings in new faces and keeps regulars up to date with what is on.
