About us

Helping local businesses excel online

RC Digital is a new web design business in Pembrokeshire. We're passionate about great design and we love helping local businesses grow, stand out and be found.

Who we are

We started RC Digital because we believe every local business deserves to look great online, not just the big names with big budgets. Too many brilliant cafés, trades, salons and shops are hard to find on Google, or rely only on a Facebook page.

We're passionate about changing that. We design and build websites, apps, logos and branding that help businesses excel: making it easy for customers to find you, trust you and get in touch.

As a new business, we give every client our full attention. You'll deal with the same friendly person from your first message to launch day and beyond.
