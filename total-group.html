Total Group Contractors Website

A multi-page website for a general and residential construction company, designed to show their work and turn visitors into quote requests.

The brief

Total Group Contractors needed a website that felt as solid and dependable as their work: clear about what they build, easy to navigate, and built to bring in quote requests.

Preview the website →

What we delivered

  • Bold, on-brand designNavy, yellow and a blueprint-style grid to suit a construction brand.
  • Services overviewNew builds, renovations and repairs, explained clearly.
  • Project logA filterable list of projects by type: new builds, renovations, additions and repairs.
  • Photo slideshowA rotating showcase of work on the home page.
  • Quote request formVisitors can describe their project and ask for a quote.
  • Mobile-friendlyA pop-out menu and layouts that work on every screen.
