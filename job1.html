Job Docket App

An app we built to take the paperwork out of the trades, so tradesmen can spend less time on admin and more time on the tools.

The brief

Tradesmen were losing hours every week to paper dockets, lost receipts and chasing signatures. The goal was simple: every job documented, in one place, from a phone.

We designed an app that fits how tradespeople actually work: quick to use on site, and without needing a desk.

What we delivered

  • Digital job docketsCreate and fill in a docket for every job in seconds.
  • Photo captureAttach before-and-after photos straight from the camera.
  • Customer sign-offClients sign on screen when the job is complete.
  • Job historyEvery docket stored and searchable, so nothing gets lost.

The result

Admin that used to happen in the van at the end of the day now happens in a few taps on site. Every job is documented, every time.
